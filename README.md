# Dashboard-
This is my project 
Nykaa Sales Dashboard 2025

Project Overview

The Nykaa Sales Dashboard 2025 is a data visualization project designed to analyze and monitor sales performance across different cities, regions, brands, product categories, and customer segments.

The dashboard provides valuable insights into sales trends, revenue distribution, customer behavior, and top-performing products to support data-driven business decisions.

Dashboard Features

1. Sales by City

- Visualizes sales distribution across different cities.
- Uses a geographical map to identify high-performing locations.
- Helps understand regional customer demand.

2. Monthly Sales Trend

- Displays monthly sales performance throughout 2025.
- Identifies seasonal patterns and sales fluctuations.
- Helps track growth and performance over time.

3. Revenue by Region

- Compares sales revenue across East, South, North, and West regions.
- Highlights regions contributing the most to overall revenue.

4. Sales by Brand

- Compares the sales performance of different beauty and skincare brands.
- Identifies top-performing brands such as Kay Beauty, Nykaa, and L'Oréal Paris.

5. Sales by Category

- Analyzes sales across categories including:
  - Skincare
  - Makeup
  - Fragrance
  - Haircare
  - Personal Care

6. Top 10 Best-Selling Products

- Highlights the top-performing products based on sales.
- Helps identify popular products and customer preferences.

7. Customer Segmentation

- Displays order status distribution, including:
  - Delivered
  - Shipped
  - Cancelled
  - Returned

Project Objective

The primary objective of this dashboard is to transform sales data into meaningful visual insights, enabling better understanding of business performance, customer purchasing patterns, and revenue trends.

Conclusion

The Nykaa Sales Dashboard 2025 provides a comprehensive overview of sales performance and customer behavior. It helps identify growth opportunities, evaluate product performance, and support strategic decision-making through interactive data visualization.
